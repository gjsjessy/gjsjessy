### Hi, Jessia here 👋

I'm a **Product Designer based in Singapore** with over 6 years of experience designing digital products and complex systems across B2B and B2C, including enterprise SaaS, healthtech, fintech, Web3, and e-commerce. I design products that don't just look good, they make complex things easier to understand and improve how people and businesses work. I enjoy turning complicated requirements, workflows, and ambiguous problems into clear, intuitive, and scalable experiences.

Through out my career, I have experience in designing product 0→1, from shaping an early idea and defining the experience, to creating pitches and proposals, validating concepts, and turning them into tangible products. I enjoy working in the space where the problem and requirements are still evolving, collaborating closely with product, engineering, founders, and stakeholders to bring ideas to life.

I currently have a chance to work at **Data4Life**, a non-profit digital health organisation, where I lead product design for one of their products, Data2Evidence (D2E), an open-source healthcare data platform built on the OHDSI ecosystem, adopted by research institutions and health systems globally. Outside of design, I love exploring new places and spending time in nature. I'm naturally curious and enjoy learning from different people, environments, and experiences, which often inspires how I approach design too.
